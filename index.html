I started in IT support — learning every OS, every quirk, every user problem. Over 17 years, that foundation grew into a specialization in network infrastructure, with a particular focus on firewalls, secure network design, and enterprise switching.

I work across Fortinet, Sophos, Unifi, and VyOS environments, building networks that are robust and maintainable. I'm honest about my stack: VyOS is powerful, but its pure CLI interface keeps me on my toes.

Outside of professional infrastructure, I have a strong passion for home automation — especially Home Assistant. I believe smart homes should be local, private, and actually reliable.

Windows, macOS, Linux — I've never had a preferred OS. All three are home.
